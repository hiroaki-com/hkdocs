# Yakubun Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

HKDOCS ("we") sets out below how user information is handled in Yakubun (訳文), an iPhone and iPad app and Safari extension (the "App").

### 1. Information we collect

The App does not collect user data. There is no account registration, and the App contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

### 2. Translation processing

Translation uses Apple's translation feature built into the iPhone or iPad and runs only on the device. The text of the page being viewed and its translation are never sent anywhere, including to us, and are never stored.

The download of translation languages is provided by Apple as a feature of iOS and iPadOS.

### 3. Information stored on the device

- Settings for the view, the size of the translated text, whether the button is tucked away, whether to show the "訳" button, the button's position, and whether translations are hidden are stored in the Safari extension's storage area.
- To keep translating, the address of the page being translated is held in the device's memory until the tab is closed. It is cleared when Safari is quit.

This information is never sent off the device. Deleting the App also deletes the saved settings.

### 4. Permission to access websites

The App's Safari extension requests access to "All Websites" so that it can translate whichever page is being viewed. It reads page content only to display the translation on that page.

### 5. Purchases and the trial

In-app purchases for the App (the "7 日間お試し" 7-day trial and "Pro"), including getting the trial, purchasing, restoring purchases, and redeeming codes, are handled by Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we never receive it.

To decide whether its features can be used, the App and its Safari extension read, on the device, the purchase records that Apple keeps on the device (when the trial was obtained, whether Pro is owned, and the version of the App that was first obtained). This information is never sent off the device and is never stored.

What we can see is limited to aggregated figures, such as the number of sales, that Apple provides in App Store Connect. They contain no information that identifies individual users.

### 6. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
