# Yakubun Support

Yakubun (訳文) is a Safari extension for iPhone that shows a Japanese translation alongside English pages opened in Safari. Translation runs on the iPhone, and the page text is never sent off the device.

[Download on the App Store (Japan)](https://apps.apple.com/jp/app/id6827465815)

- Requirements: iPhone (iOS 26.4 or later)
- Translation: English → Japanese
- Views: between lines (行間), paragraph (段落), word (単語), translation only (全文)
- Availability: Japanese App Store only. The app's interface is in Japanese.
- [Privacy Policy](/yakubun/privacy/)

### Getting started

1. Open the Yakubun app and tap "言語をダウンロード" (Download languages) to install the English and Japanese translation languages (first time only; requires a network connection).
2. Tap "Safari の設定を開く" (Open Safari Settings), turn on the "訳文" extension, and allow it on all websites.
3. Open an English page in Safari, tap "訳" at the bottom left, and choose a view (行間, 段落, 単語, or 全文).

Tapping "終了" (Quit) removes the translations and the menu and restores the page.

### Frequently asked questions

#### I can't download the languages

Make sure you are connected to the network, then tap "言語をダウンロード" in the app again. If the app says that English → Japanese translation isn't available on this device (この端末では英語 → 日本語の翻訳を使えません), the app can't be used on that device.

#### The extension won't turn on, or the settings won't open

Open the iPhone's Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → "訳文", turn it on, and allow it on all websites.

#### The "訳" button doesn't appear

- The "訳" button doesn't appear on pages that are not in English.
- If you just turned on the extension, reload the page.
- In the extension menu in Safari's address bar, check that the extension is allowed on the site.
- In version 1.0.1 or later, open "訳文" from the button at the left end of Safari's address bar and check that "「訳」のボタンを出す" (Show the "訳" button) is on.

#### The button disappeared

In version 1.0.1 or later, you can tuck the "訳" button and the floating button away by swiping them off the side of the screen. Tap the tab left at the edge to bring them back.

#### Some parts aren't translated

Navigation, code, and input fields are intentionally left untranslated. Depending on how a page is built, parts of the main text may not be translated.

#### The translation sounds unnatural

Translations come from the iPhone's built-in translation feature and may sound unnatural depending on the context.

#### Can I use it without a network connection?

Once the languages are downloaded, translation itself needs no network connection. Pages that are already loaded can be translated even in Airplane Mode.
