# Darts Calc App Support

Darts Calc App (ダーツ計算アプリ) is an iPhone and iPad app that keeps darts scores with one tap per dart. No account registration is needed. There is also a free [darts score calculator](/darts/) that runs in the browser.

[Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/apple-store/id6838596154?pt=129688386&mt=8&ct=hkdocs-darts-support-en)

- Requirements: iPhone (iOS 26 or later) or iPad (iPadOS 26 or later)
- Games: Count-Up, X01 (301–1501), Cricket, Medley, and 2 vs 2 team play
- Languages: English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, and Korean
- Pricing: Count-Up is free. The other games, Records & Trends, and syncing records between iPhone and iPad require Pro (a yearly or monthly subscription, or Lifetime, a one-time purchase).
- [Privacy Policy](/darts/app/privacy/)

### How to use

1. On the home screen, choose a game, set the number of players and the rules (the X01 starting score, the out rule, the bull score, and so on), and tap Start. The App remembers the settings you last started with.
2. Tap a multiplier (Single, Double, or Triple) and a number to enter each dart. Tap Miss when you miss the board.
3. If you make a mistake, tap Undo to take back one entry at a time. You can undo even after the game has ended.
4. When a game ends, the App shows stats such as 3DA, PPD, and MPR. Games played solo are saved to Records & Trends.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Pro doesn't work after I changed or reinstalled my device

Tap Restore Purchases on the Pro purchase screen. Pro comes back if you are signed in with the Apple Account you used to buy it.

#### I want to cancel my subscription

You can cancel from Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. While you are subscribed, you can also open it from Manage Subscription on the App's home screen. If you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period, you will not be charged for the next period. Lifetime is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew or cancel.

#### My games aren't saved

Only games finished solo are saved to Records & Trends. Games with two or more players, Medley, and team games are not saved.

#### Are Count-Up records saved on the free version?

Yes. Count-Up games finished solo are saved on the device even before you go Pro, and with Pro you can see their trend and history, including past games.

#### I want to delete a record

In the list in Records & Trends, swipe left on the record you want to delete.

#### Records don't sync between my iPhone and iPad

Syncing is a Pro feature. Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices and make sure iCloud is available. Syncing may take a little while.

#### I don't want to send usage statistics

Turn off "Send Usage Statistics" in the menu (the gear) at the top right of the home screen, and the App will not send usage statistics. What is sent is described in Section 2 of the [Privacy Policy](/darts/app/privacy/).

#### Can I use it offline?

Scoring and records work offline. Purchasing and restoring Pro and syncing with iCloud need a connection.
