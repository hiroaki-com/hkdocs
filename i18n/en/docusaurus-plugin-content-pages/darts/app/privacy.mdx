# Darts Calc App Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

HKDOCS ("we") sets out below how user information is handled in Darts Calc App (ダーツ計算アプリ), an iPhone and iPad app (the "App").

### 1. Information we do not collect

The App has no login or account registration. It never collects names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details. Player names entered in a game's settings are used only during that game and are never stored on the device or in iCloud.

The App shows no ads. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

### 2. Usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the App, we use the following two Firebase services provided by Google and send information that does not identify you personally. It is never linked to an account or a name.

- **Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)**: an app instance identifier (a value created for each device the App is installed on; it changes when the App is deleted), that a game was started or finished (the game type and number of players), that the purchase screen was opened or closed, the type of product purchased, the device and OS type, and the approximate country or region estimated from the IP address. Scores and player names are never sent.
- **Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)**: a record of when the App quits unexpectedly (where it happened, the device and OS type, the App's version, and so on).

The information sent is stored on Google's servers, and we see only aggregated results and crash records. Analytics data is deleted according to the Google Analytics data retention setting (at most 14 months), and Crashlytics reports after a 90-day retention period. For how Google handles information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

You can stop usage statistics at any time by turning off "Send Usage Statistics" in the menu at the top right of the home screen (version 1.1 and later). If you use the App Store in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your permission the first time you open it and sends nothing until you allow it. Crash reports are sent in every region so that the App can run reliably.

### 3. Information stored on the device and in iCloud

- Records of games finished solo (when the game ended, the game type and settings, and the darts thrown) are stored on the device for Records & Trends. Games with two or more players, Medley, and team games are not stored.
- The appearance mode (System, Light, or Dark), the settings last used to start each game (version 1.1 and later; player names are not included), and your choice about sending usage statistics are stored on the device. They are not synced to iCloud.
- If you use Pro, game records are stored in your own iCloud (the private area of Apple's CloudKit) and synced between the iPhone and iPad signed in to the same Apple Account. This area belongs only to you, and we cannot see it.

Records can be deleted one at a time from the list in Records & Trends. Deleting the App also deletes the records and settings on the device. Records stored in iCloud can be deleted from Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > storage management on your iPhone or iPad.

### 4. Purchases

In-app purchases for the App (Pro yearly and monthly subscriptions and Lifetime), including purchasing, renewal, cancellation, and restoring purchases, are handled by Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we never receive it.

To decide whether Pro can be used, the App reads, on the device, the purchase records that Apple keeps on the device. When a purchase succeeds, only the type of product purchased is sent as usage statistics, as described in Section 2.

What we can see in App Store Connect is limited to aggregated figures provided by Apple, such as the number of sales and subscriptions. They contain no information that identifies individual users.

### 5. For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and similar laws, we provide the following information.

- **Controller**: HKDOCS (see Section 7 for contact details)
- **Purposes and legal bases**: Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are sent on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are sent on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing the App reliably (Article 6(1)(f)). Both are used only to improve the App.
- **Recipients and international transfers**: The information is sent to Google (Firebase) and may be processed outside the EEA, including in the United States. Google transfers it under its certification to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Retention**: As described in Section 2 (usage statistics for at most 14 months, crash reports for 90 days).
- **Your rights**: You may request access to, rectification or erasure of, restriction of processing of, and portability of your information, and you may object to processing. You can withdraw consent to usage statistics at any time with the switch described in Section 2 (this does not affect sending before the withdrawal). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country.
- We do not hold your name, contact details, or the App's instance identifier and cannot identify your information, so we may be unable to fulfil some requests. Deleting the App changes the identifier, and it is no longer linked to earlier information.
- We make no decisions that affect you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

### 6. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
