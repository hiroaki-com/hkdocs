# Interlinear Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026

HKDOCS ("we") sets out below how user information is handled in Interlinear, an iPhone and iPad app and Safari extension (the "App").

### 1. Information we do not collect

The App has no login or account registration. It never collects names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details.

The App shows no ads. It does not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track users across other companies' apps or websites.

### 2. Translation processing

Translation uses Apple's translation feature built into the iPhone or iPad and runs only on the device. The text of the page being viewed, its translation, the page address, and the site name are never sent anywhere, including to us, and are never stored. The part of the page text used to identify the page's language is also handled only on the device.

The Safari extension makes no network connections. The download of translation languages is provided by Apple as a feature of iOS and iPadOS.

### 3. Usage statistics and crash reports

To improve the App, the app itself (not the Safari extension) uses the following two Firebase services provided by Google and sends information that does not identify you personally. It is never linked to an account or a name.

- **Firebase Analytics (usage statistics)**: an app instance identifier (a value created for each device the App is installed on; it changes when the App is deleted), the app's screens and how far you got in Getting Started, the languages you chose to translate into and from, that the Pro section was shown, that the trial was obtained, that Pro was purchased, or that purchases were restored, the number of translations per day, per view (Lines, Para, Words, or Full), and per language pair, the device and OS type, and the approximate country or region estimated from the IP address. The content of the pages or text you translate is never sent.
- **Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)**: a record of when the App quits unexpectedly (where it happened, the device and OS type, the App's version, and so on).

The information sent is stored on Google's servers, and we see only aggregated results and crash records. Analytics data is deleted according to the Google Analytics data retention setting (at most 14 months), and Crashlytics reports after a 90-day retention period. For how Google handles information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

You can stop usage statistics at any time by turning off "Share usage statistics" in the Privacy section of the app. If you use the App Store in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your permission the first time you open it and sends nothing until you allow it. Crash reports are sent in every region so that the App can run reliably.

### 4. Information stored on the device

- The languages to translate into and from are stored in an area on the device shared by the app and its Safari extension (an App Group).
- The number of translations (only counts per day, view, and language pair; no page addresses or text) is stored in the same area until the app sends it as described in Section 3, and is deleted once sent. Counts older than 30 days are deleted without being sent.
- Settings for the view, the size of the translated text, whether translations are hidden, whether the button is tucked away, whether to show the button, and the button's position are stored in the Safari extension's storage area.
- Your choice about sending usage statistics is stored in the app.
- To keep translating, the address of the page being translated is held in the device's memory until the tab is closed. It is cleared when Safari is quit.

Except for the translation counts in Section 3, this information is never sent off the device. Deleting the App also deletes the saved settings.

### 5. Permission to access websites

The App's Safari extension requests access to "All Websites" so that it can translate whichever page is being viewed. It reads page content only to display the translation on that page and to identify the page's language.

### 6. Purchases and the trial

In-app purchases for the App (the 7-day trial and Pro), including getting the trial, purchasing, restoring purchases, and redeeming codes, are handled by Apple's App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple, and we never receive it.

To decide whether its features can be used, the App and its Safari extension read, on the device, the purchase records that Apple keeps on the device (when the trial was obtained and whether Pro is owned). These records are never sent off the device and are never stored. Only the fact that the trial was obtained, that Pro was purchased, or that purchases were restored is sent as usage statistics, as described in Section 3.

What we can see in App Store Connect is limited to aggregated figures provided by Apple, such as the number of sales. They contain no information that identifies individual users.

### 7. For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, and similar laws, we provide the following information.

- **Controller**: HKDOCS (see Section 9 for contact details)
- **Purposes and legal bases**: Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) are sent on the basis of your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are sent on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing the App reliably (Article 6(1)(f)). Both are used only to improve the App.
- **Recipients and international transfers**: The information is sent to Google (Firebase) and may be processed outside the EEA, including in the United States. Google transfers it under its certification to the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
- **Retention**: As described in Section 3 (usage statistics for at most 14 months, crash reports for 90 days).
- **Your rights**: You may request access to, rectification or erasure of, restriction of processing of, and portability of your information, and you may object to processing. You can withdraw consent to usage statistics at any time with the switch described in Section 3 (this does not affect sending before the withdrawal). You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country.
- We do not hold your name, contact details, or the App's instance identifier and cannot identify your information, so we may be unable to fulfil some requests. Deleting the App changes the identifier, and it is no longer linked to earlier information.
- We make no decisions that affect you based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

### 8. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy as needed. The revised policy takes effect when it is posted on this page.
