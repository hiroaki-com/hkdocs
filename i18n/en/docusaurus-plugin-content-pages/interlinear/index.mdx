# Interlinear Support

Interlinear is a Safari extension for iPhone and iPad that shows a translation in the language you choose alongside pages opened in Safari. Translation runs on the device, and the page text is never sent off the device.

- Requirements: iPhone (iOS 26.4 or later) or iPad (iPadOS 26.4 or later)
- Languages: translates between any pair of English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Polish, Vietnamese, Indonesian, and Thai
- Views: Lines, Para, Words (English pages only), and Full
- Pricing: free to try for 7 days; to keep using it after that, Pro (a one-time purchase) is required.
- Availability: every App Store region except mainland China. The app is available in all 16 languages above.
- [Privacy Policy](/interlinear/privacy/)

### Getting started

1. Open the Interlinear app and, in "Choose languages", choose the language to translate into and the languages to translate from. Choosing a language downloads its translation data (requires a network connection).
2. Tap "Open Safari Settings", turn on the "Interlinear" extension, and allow it on all websites.
3. In "Start the free trial" in the app, get the 7-day trial.
4. Open a page in a language you chose in Safari, tap the round button at the bottom left, and choose a view (Lines, Para, Words, or Full).

Tapping "Exit" removes the translations and the button and restores the page.

The labels on the button and in the app follow your device's language. In English, the button reads "Tr" and, while translating, shows the view ("L", "P", "W", "F", "LW", or "PW").

### Frequently asked questions

#### I can't download the languages

Make sure you are connected to the network, then tap "Download" on the Languages screen in the app again. The download continues in the background. If a language shows "Not available", that language can't be translated on the device.

#### I want to change the languages

Open "Choose languages" under "Getting Started" in the app to change them at any time. Changing the language to translate into downloads the data again for the languages you translate from.

#### The extension won't turn on, or the settings won't open

Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad → Apps → Safari → Extensions → "Interlinear", turn it on, and allow it on all websites.

#### A message says "The translation languages aren't downloaded"

The data for the pair of the page's language and the language you translate into isn't on the device yet. Tap the message to open the app and download it on the Languages screen.

#### A message says "Tap to open the Interlinear app and finish Getting Started"

This appears before you get the trial. Tap the message to open the app, then get the trial in "Start the free trial" under "Getting Started", or purchase Pro.

#### A message says "Your trial has ended"

The trial ends 7 days after you get it, and all views stop. To keep using the app, tap the message to open the app and purchase Pro.

#### Pro doesn't work after switching devices or reinstalling

Tap "Restore Purchases" under "Getting Started" in the app. Pro comes back if you are signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Reinstalling does not restart the trial.

#### The button doesn't appear

- The button appears only on pages in the languages you chose to translate from. It doesn't appear on pages in the language you translate into.
- If you just turned on the extension, reload the page.
- In the extension menu in Safari's address bar, check that the extension is allowed on the site.
- Open "Interlinear" from the button at the left end of Safari's address bar and check that "Show the button" is on.

#### I can't tap Words

Words currently works on English pages only. On pages in other languages, use Lines, Para, or Full.

#### The button disappeared

You can tuck the button away by swiping it off the side of the screen. Tap the tab left at the edge to bring it back.

#### Some parts aren't translated

Navigation, code, and input fields are intentionally left untranslated. Paragraphs in languages you didn't choose to translate from, and paragraphs already in the language you translate into, are not translated either. Depending on how a page is built, parts of the main text may not be translated.

#### The translation sounds unnatural

Translations come from the device's built-in translation feature and may sound unnatural depending on the context and the language pair.

#### Can I use it without a network connection?

Once the languages are downloaded, translation itself needs no network connection. Pages that are already loaded can be translated even in Airplane Mode.
